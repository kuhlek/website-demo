Services

Flexible delivery models for complex projects.

Whether you want a fixed-price contract, an integrated design-and-build team or hands-on construction management, we structure our service around your risk, budget and timeline.

General contracting

We take full responsibility for building your project to the approved design, managing every trade, supplier and inspection under one contract.

Design and build

One team and one contract from concept to completion, giving you earlier cost certainty and fewer interfaces to manage.

Construction management

We manage the programme, procurement and trades on your behalf, with open-book transparency and full cost visibility.

Interior fit-outs

Offices, clinics, retail and hospitality interiors delivered fast and to a high specification, often in live buildings.

Refurbishment and upgrades

Phased works in occupied buildings, with strict control of noise, dust, access and safety.

Civil and external works

Earthworks, roads, parking, drainage and landscaping that support the building and last.

Pre-construction and estimating

Early budgeting, buildability reviews and value engineering that protect your budget before design is fixed.

Maintenance and defects

Responsive defect rectification and planned maintenance so your facility keeps performing after handover.

Included in every project

  • A named project manager and site team
  • A detailed programme with critical path
  • Weekly progress, cost, risk and safety reports
  • A site-specific health and safety plan
  • Quality plans, inspections and independent testing
  • Full close-out documentation and warranties

How a project runs

  1. Briefing. We study your requirements, site conditions and constraints.
  2. Tender and planning. You receive method, programme, price and safety plan.
  3. Mobilisation. Approvals, procurement and site establishment.
  4. Construction. Daily supervision, weekly reports and open communication.
  5. Commissioning. Systems tested and witnessed with your team.
  6. Handover. Snags closed, documents delivered, defects period begins.
