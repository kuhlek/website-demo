Safety and quality

Zero harm. Zero compromise.

Our commitment to zero harm

Every person who enters our sites, whether employee, trade, visitor or neighbour, has the right to go home safe. We treat safety as a leadership responsibility and a condition of doing business, not a checklist.

Safe sites are well-planned sites. That is why strong safety performance and reliable programmes go hand in hand.

Leadership

Directors attend site safety reviews and own the health and safety plan.

Compliance

We operate under the Occupational Health and Safety Act and the Construction Regulations.

How we keep sites safe

Risk assessments

Every task is assessed and planned before work begins, with a safe method statement.

Inductions and training

Everyone is inducted and trained for the risks of their work before they start.

Daily toolbox talks

Teams brief every morning on the day's work, hazards and controls.

Inspections and audits

Regular internal and independent audits, with actions tracked to closure.

Incident reporting

Every incident and near miss is reported, investigated and learned from.

Protection of the public

Hoarding, traffic control and dust and noise management protect your neighbours and customers.

Quality you can verify

We build quality in rather than inspect it in at the end. Each project has a quality plan with inspection and test plans, hold points and approvals, so you can see the standard being met at every stage.

  • Inspection and test plans for each work package
  • Hold points witnessed by your representative
  • Independent testing of concrete, waterproofing and services
  • Photographic records and digital snag tracking
  • A complete close-out pack at handover

Environmental responsibility

We minimise waste, protect watercourses and neighbours, source responsibly and separate and recycle site waste wherever we can.
