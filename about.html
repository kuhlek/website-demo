About us

A contractor you can hold to its word.

Our story

We started with a simple conviction: clients are let down less by difficult construction than by poor communication, hidden costs and weak site management. We set out to build a contractor that fixes those three things first.

As a women-led company, we bring a different standard of detail, planning and care to an industry that rewards both. We run our sites the way we would want our own projects run: organised, safe, transparent and finished properly.

Today we deliver healthcare, commercial, industrial and institutional work for clients who value reliability over promises.

Our mission

To build safe, high-quality facilities that serve the people who use them, delivered on programme and to budget with complete transparency from tender to handover.

Our vision

To be the contractor clients appoint again and recommend first, because we did exactly what we said we would do.

Our values

Integrity

We price honestly, report honestly and own our mistakes, because trust is the only thing that carries a project through difficulty.

Zero harm

Every person on and around our sites goes home safe. No programme, price or deadline is worth an injury.

Quality

Good work is in the details nobody sees as well as those they do. We inspect at every hold point, not just at the end.

Communication

You will always know what is happening, what happens next and what it costs. Bad news travels fast with us, and early.

Accountability

We keep our word on scope, programme and standards, and we put our name to every handover.

Respect

For our people, our trades, your neighbours and the environment we build in.

What we provide with every tender

Serious clients ask for proof. We provide it up front, so you can appoint with confidence.

  • Company registration and tax compliance documents
  • Contractor registration and grading certificate
  • COIDA letter of good standing
  • Contract works and public liability insurance certificates
  • Health and safety plan and appointed safety officers
  • Quality plan with inspection and test plans
  • References from comparable projects
