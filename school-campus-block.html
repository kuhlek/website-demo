School Campus Block

Education

Project overview

A new classroom and administration block with science labs, a library and covered walkways.

The challenge

Building beside active classrooms, with strict safeguarding and access rules.

Our approach

Separate site access, a gated working area and work scheduled around school terms kept learners safe and lessons uninterrupted.

The result

The project was handed over with a complete close-out pack of as-built drawings, certificates, manuals and warranties, and a snag list closed out with the client.
