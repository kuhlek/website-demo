Questions clients ask before they appoint us

Can you show experience on projects like ours?

Yes. We share comparable project case studies and put you in touch with referees on request, so you can speak to clients who had similar requirements.

How do you keep our operations running during construction?

We plan access, noise, dust and out-of-hours work with your team before we start, and agree a communication routine so there are no surprises on the day.

How do you control cost and variations?

Our tenders are fully itemised. Any change is priced and approved in writing before the work proceeds, and you receive a cost report every week.

What insurance and compliance documents do you provide?

With every tender we provide our company documents, contractor registration, tax and COIDA compliance, insurance certificates and our health and safety plan.

What happens after handover?

You receive as-built drawings, certificates, manuals and warranties. We close out a snag list with you and remain responsible for defects during the defects liability period.

Do you work with our consultants and designers?

Yes. We collaborate with your architects and engineers or provide a design-and-build route, whichever suits your project and risk preference.

Tell us about your site. We respond within one business day with a clear next step and a named contact.
